## What is Gil's Garage Anyway?

Gil's Garage is my foray into building something not only useful for learning and expressing the skills I've learned, but also exemplary of that pursuit in and of itself. My next post is planned to be on the architecture and system design of the garage, and how I've made it secure by default with a tiny attack surface!

For the CliffsNotes, this site uses a from-scratch Go static site generator to populate HTML pages for consumption by AWS CloudFront. It was remarkably satisfying to set up, complete with automated deploys with each push of fresh Markdown content.

Join me here as I work towards the cutting edge of cloud and AI security.

## About the Author

My name is Jacob Gilhaus, and I am a security engineer working to secure an expansive set of products at a major tech company. While the team has a vast scope, I specialize in leveraging the cloud to securely build automation and services that further our mission. This chapter of my career has led to me learning cloud, IaC, containerization, and artificial intelligence largely from scratch.

I started at a small liberal arts school where I was a captain of the baseball team studying physics with a minor in math. It soon became apparent that while I deeply enjoyed the intellectual and thought-provoking nature of physics, especially quantum physics, I was not uniquely or supremely talented enough to push this field forward. I took computer science out of interest my sophomore year, intrigued to learn about what my parents did (a project manager and a Kubernetes operator) and how such a fast-paced field worked. The possibilities seemed endless.

I transferred to an engineering school and quickly devoured every course I could get my hands on in computer engineering, computer science, and a bit later cybersecurity. It was immediately obvious to me that while building websites was fun, there was so much more that fascinated me in how computers worked at their core, and that the history of computing meant that there was huge opportunity in security. 

The internet wasn't designed with security as a first principle, and the imbalance between attackers and defenders opens up numerous unanswered questions about how we keep data secure. 

I studied computer architecture and networking. I only got as far as an introduction to AI (pre-ChatGPT) and wasn't able to land a course in cloud (it filled up fast!) by the time I graduated with an MS in Computer Engineering. My early career made those gaps clear, but also provided extensive opportunities to learn as I applied the principles of computing from college. Building from that foundation, cloud and IaC were a natural next layer up.

Today I am a Senior Security Engineer working with AI, cloud services, code, and automation daily to manage vulnerabilities at scale. I am still as hungry to learn as ever, and Go, CI/CD, GitOps, and integrating and securing AI workflows are some of my current projects in and out of the office.

That's what the garage is for! So until next time, I wish you well. Or should I say -- as a new father...
